Delete your Pets24x7 account

You can delete your account yourself at any time, from the app or the website. It takes effect straight away.

You are signed in as .

How to delete it

  1. Sign in: pet parents or businesses.
  2. Come back to this page, or open My Dashboard → Account (businesses: Settings).
  3. Choose Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm.

Can't sign in? Email support@pets24x7.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Pet parents: your profile, pets, saved businesses, membership and sign-in details.
  • Businesses: your business account, services, campaigns and Featured placements. Your public listing stays in the directory as unclaimed.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment and invoice records, as required by tax law, without a link to your account.
  • Enquiries and reviews you sent to businesses, without your account attached.
  • A backup copy for up to 180 days so an account deleted by mistake can be restored on request; after that it is gone for good.
