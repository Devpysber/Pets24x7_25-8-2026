Common questions

How are payments processed?

Securely via Razorpay — UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking and wallets all supported. We don’t store your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

There is nothing to cancel: plans don’t renew by themselves. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and we remind you 7, 3 and 1 day before it ends so you can renew in one tap.

How does the concierge work?

Message us on WhatsApp from the number on your account with what you need, where and when. We shortlist businesses near you, check availability and, on Silver and Gold, book it and confirm with you.

Do you offer refunds?

Within 7 days of purchase if you haven’t used the concierge yet, message us on WhatsApp at +91 99300 90487.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your pet and we’ll suggest the right one. No obligation.
